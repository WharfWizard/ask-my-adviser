Privacy

Ask My Adviser is provided by the Academy of Life Planning. It is designed to hold as little about you as possible.

Your documents

  • Files you add are opened and read in your own web browser. The files themselves are never uploaded.
  • Before anything is sent, your browser masks common identifiers — National Insurance numbers, account, policy and reference numbers, sort codes, card numbers, IBANs, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers and postcodes — and any names or words you list. Masking is automatic and may miss things, so you can check and edit the text first.
  • When you ask for an analysis, the masked text, any notes you write about your goals, and your choices are sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the results. They are not saved by this app or written to our logs.
  • Anthropic processes this data to provide the service under its commercial terms. For how Anthropic handles API data, see anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Your results

  • Results exist only in your browser tab. Closing or refreshing the tab clears them. Print or save them as a PDF if you want to keep them.

What we do keep

  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs such as the time of a request and error codes. These do not include your document text.
  • To limit misuse, the server keeps a short-lived count of analyses per visitor each day, using a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address. It is held in memory only and cleared daily.

Good practice

  • Upload only what is necessary. Remove unnecessary information about other people.
  • Never include passwords or access codes in your documents.

Questions about privacy: please contact the Academy of Life Planning.
